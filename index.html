Benefits

ETHICS 4 BENEFIT is a new concept to boost the inter-cooperation between parties, including merchantors which typically trade alone and do not have the resources needed to grow. We offer innovative “brick & mortar” resources powered by a digital platform to bring, with glamour, sustainable products to the clientele that values them. Our common goal is to contribute to the change we all want to see in the world.

Digital Platform

ETHICS 4 BENEFIT is powered by a modern digital platform enabling the resources and functionalities that Cooperators need independently of their role in this community.

Connection

ETHICS 4 BENEFIT connects cooperators with different roles in the society, such as end-users, sustainable Traders (e.g., brands, retailers, agents), non-profit organizations and investors.
ETHICS 4 BENEFIT helps all cooperators to focus on the same goal we already have, that is to build a sustainable and better world.

Community

The ETHICS 4 BENEFIT is an initiative to connect all cooperators that want to be the change we all want to see in the world. Circular economy is key and engaged cooperators in their society roles are the enablers. 
Our commitment is to join forces and build a strong community to drive the change.

Consumers

Cooperators with Consumer profile who register as members of ETHICS 4 BENEFIT will enjoy an enjoyable shopping experience either online or offline. As a community consumer, your ideas and support are welcome and appreciated. ETHICS 4 BENEFIT makes a difference for tomorrow.

Traders

Trader Cooperators (e.g. brands, retailers, suppliers, non-profit organizations) offering sustainable products / services that adhere to the ETHICS 4 BENEFIT community will minimize their stocks and selling risk and other advantages.

About

ETHICS 4 BENEFIT is a community built by and for cooperators that share the common wish to be the change we all want to see in the world. Our motivation and engagement while sharing and trading sustainable resources will enable circular economy and will make the world a fair and better place to live, with respect for people and planet.

Our Roots

ETHICS 4 BENEFIT was born from the dreams of pioneering sustainable designers and retailers. Driven by a passion for sustainable craftsmanship, we saw the struggles of smaller, ethics-driven brands while large corporations prioritized profit over people and planet. ETHICS 4 BENEFIT understood it was time to unite and drive the change we all want to see in the world.

Our Skills

ETHICS 4 BENEFIT core team brings together a rich blend of talents and expertise, spanning environmental sustainability, design, fashion, ICT, project management, marketing, sales, legal and finance. For specialized investment projects, we tap into our community of skilled cooperators, inviting experts to support them.

Roadmap

ETHICS 4 BENEFIT is an ambitious movement uniting sustainable designers, producers, consumers and investors to drive circular economy and create real change. Our roadmap is a dynamic journey, achieving milestone after milestone while keeping our ultimate vision in focus. Reach out to us to discover more about the timeline and join the community
